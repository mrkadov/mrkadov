## Style

```
Emotional cinematic boom bap, 90 BPM. Melancholic piano as the main melodic identity, distant atmospheric violin as emotional support, dusty organic 90s boom bap drums with restrained swing and a head-nod pocket, warm deep bass. Intimate male rap vocal, close to the microphone in the verses, wider and more emotional in the chorus. Begin lonely and spacious: sparse piano and distant violin, no drums yet. Late-night car interior, windshield condensation, low dashboard light, empty passenger seat. Verses stay intimate with dusty kick, snappy snare, swung hats. Chorus expands: wider drums, swelling strings, emotional peak. Verse 2 returns to a controlled pocket. Final chorus is the biggest, widest section. Outro slowly strips the instruments away like the car rolling to a stop. Dark, nocturnal, melancholic, professional mix, warm low end. Strictly organic boom bap drum programming, dusty samples, restrained swing.
```
