# Gelme Bu Gece — Suno Custom Mode

Duygusal boom bap, 90 BPM. Custom Mode’da **Style** ve **Lyrics** ayrı yapıştırılır. Style’a söz yazılmaz.

Kimlik: 2–4 unsur.

| Rol | Unsur |
| --- | --- |
| Ana | melancholic piano |
| Destek | distant atmospheric violin |
| Groove | dusty 90s boom bap drums, restrained swing |
| Gövde | warm deep bass |
| Vokal | intimate male rap, close-mic verse, wider chorus |

---

## 1. Title

```
Gelme Bu Gece
```

## 2. Slider

Ticari / kontrollü take:

- **Weirdness:** düşük–orta (yaklaşık 20–35)
- **Style Influence:** yüksek (yaklaşık 70–85)

İyi sonuç yoksa önce slider’ı, sonra tek kelimeyi değiştir.

## 3. Style of Music

Etiket yığını değil, müzikal hikâye. Önemli kelimeler başta.

```
Emotional cinematic boom bap, 90 BPM. Melancholic piano as the main melodic identity, distant atmospheric violin as emotional support, dusty organic 90s boom bap drums with restrained swing and a head-nod pocket, warm deep bass. Intimate male rap vocal, close to the microphone in the verses, wider and more emotional in the chorus. Begin lonely and spacious: sparse piano and distant violin, no drums yet. Verses stay intimate with dusty kick, snappy snare, swung hats. Chorus expands: wider drums, swelling strings, emotional peak. Verse 2 returns to a controlled pocket. Final chorus is the biggest, widest section. Outro slowly strips the instruments away. Dark, nocturnal, melancholic, professional mix, warm low end. Strictly organic boom bap drum programming, dusty samples, restrained swing.
```

Kısa yedek (karakter limiti daralırsa):

```
Emotional cinematic boom bap, 90 BPM, melancholic piano, distant violin, dusty organic 90s drums with restrained swing, warm bass, intimate male rap vocal. Lonely intro, spacious verses, wide emotional chorus, sparse outro. Dark, nocturnal, professional mix, warm low end.
```

## 5. Enerji eğrisi

Style’ın içinde duruyor. Ayrı not olarak da kilit:

```
0:00 intro — piyano + uzaktan keman, davul yok
Verse — close-mic, dusty pocket, kontrollü
Chorus — geniş davul, şişen keman, duygusal tepe
Verse 2 — kontrollü enerji, biraz daha dolu
Bridge — davul düşer
Final chorus — en geniş
Outro — enstrümanlar tek tek çekilir
```

## 6. Beat’i önce kilitle (isteğe bağlı)

Nakarat melodisinden önce **enstrümantal boom bap** al. Aynı Style, Lyrics yerine:

```
[Intro]
[Sparse melancholic piano, distant violin, no drums]

[Verse]
[Dusty 90 BPM boom bap enters: dusty kick, snappy snare, swung hats, warm bass, piano continues, instrumental]

[Chorus]
[Wider drums, swelling violin, emotional peak, still instrumental]

[Verse]
[Return to restrained head-nod pocket]

[Bridge]
[Drums drop, piano and violin]

[Final Chorus]
[Biggest dusty drums and strings]

[Outro]
[Strip away instruments]
```

Beğendiğin beat’i omurga yap. Sonra aynı Style + vokal Lyrics ile Cover / Reuse.

## 7. Reuse mikro seti

Aynı söz, aynı yapı. Yalnızca Style’da **tek satır** değiştir:

```
A: darker, more nocturnal, heavier low end
B: tighter dusty boom bap pocket, more head-nod snare
C: more melancholic piano, less drums in the verses
D: calmer intimate vocal, closer to the microphone
E: more cinematic chorus, bigger strings, widest drums
```

İlk hedef nakarat. Verse kötü, kanca iyiyse o take omurgadır.
