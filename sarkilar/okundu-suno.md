HIT tesliminin KA-1 katmanı. Style’a söz yok. Lyrics’te İngilizce bölüm etiketi var.

## Title

```
Okundu

## Slider

- Weirdness: 20–35 (ticari, güvenli lane)
- Style Influence: 70–85
- Kötü sonuçta önce slider, sonra tek değişken

## Style

```
Emotional cinematic boom bap, 90 BPM. Melancholic piano as the main melodic identity, distant atmospheric violin as emotional support, dusty organic 90s boom bap drums with restrained swing and a head-nod pocket, warm deep bass. Intimate male rap vocal, close to the microphone in the verses, wider and more emotional in the chorus. Begin lonely and spacious: sparse piano and distant violin, no drums yet. Cold night interior, dim kitchen light. Verses stay intimate with dusty kick, snappy snare, swung hats. Chorus expands: wider drums, swelling strings, emotional peak. Verse 2 returns to a controlled pocket. Final chorus is the biggest, widest section. Outro slowly strips the instruments away. Dark, nocturnal, melancholic, professional mix, warm low end. Strictly organic boom bap drum programming, dusty samples, restrained swing.
```

## Beat kilidi (enstrümantal)

```

[Wider drums, swelling violin, emotional peak, still instrumental]

[Return to restrained head-nod pocket]

[Bridge]
[Drums drop, piano and violin]

[Biggest dusty drums and strings]

## Reuse mikro seti

```
A: darker, more nocturnal, heavier low end
B: tighter dusty boom bap pocket, more head-nod snare
C: shorter chorus, fewer words, more space
D: calmer intimate vocal, closer to the microphone
E: more cinematic chorus, bigger strings, widest drums
```

İlk kilit hâlâ nakarat. `Okundu` zayıf düşerse take’i çoğaltma; kancayı veya melodiyi değiştir.
